## EPA 2 — Differential Diagnosis Quality
**Max Score:** 20 | **Data Source:** End-of-Stage-1 learner statement

### Scoring Rubric
| Level | Score | Behavioral Indicators |
|-------|-------|-----------------------|
| Excellent | 17–20 | ≥3 diagnoses ranked by priority, dangerous condition included, justified |
| Good | 13–16 | ≥2 diagnoses, correct organ system, missing one dangerous DDx |
| Fair | 9–12 | Single diagnosis only (Premature Closure) |
| Poor | 0–8 | Contradicts clinical evidence, wrong organ system |

### Penalties
- Premature Closure: cap EPA 2 at 12 if only 1 DDx offered
- Dangerous Disease Omission: -4 if no dangerous DDx listed (when score ≥13)

### Required Dangerous Conditions (abdominal pain cases)
- Mesenteric Ischemia, Aortic Dissection, Inferior MI
